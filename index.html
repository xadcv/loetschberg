Primary font

A glyf-flavoured variable font carries ss01, ss02, COLRv1, CPAL, and outline deltas for all colour layers. Unsupported colour renderers still receive a usable monochrome outline.

Text sidecar

A CFF2 variable OTF keeps the base and handdrawn forms for pure text workflows, with colour tables intentionally removed.

Figma compatibility pair

Regular and Extruded are separate, palette-free glyf families for applications without COLRv1 rendering. Both retain weight, width, and Handdrawn through ss01.
